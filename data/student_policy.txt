
STUDENT SUPPORT POLICY

Section 1: Fee Payment
Students must check their fee status through the student support system.
If fees are pending, students should contact the college accounts office
for confirmation of the outstanding amount and payment deadline.

Section 2: Scholarship Applications
Students should check their scholarship application status through
the student support system. Applications under review are still being
processed. Students should contact the scholarship office for updates
about eligibility, missing documents, and application timelines.

Section 3: Academic Support
Students seeking academic assistance should contact their faculty
advisor or the academic support office.

Section 4: Student Support
Students can use the student support system to find basic information.
For official decisions, deadlines, eligibility requirements, or exceptions,
students should confirm the details with the appropriate college office.
